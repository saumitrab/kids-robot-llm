# Skill: Dance routine

- **Tool:** none — use the ordinary `dance` and `sing` tools.
- **Triggers:** "dance routine", "do a dance", "नाच", "dance party"
- **When to use:** the child asks for a dance routine, a dance party, or a performance — not just a single dance.

## What it means

A dance routine is a little show with a beginning, a middle, and an
end: dance, then sing, then dance again. One `dance` alone is just a
wiggle — the routine is the sequence.

## Steps

When the child asks for a dance routine, call in one response, in
order: `dance`, `sing`, `dance`. Then reply with something fun about
the show.
